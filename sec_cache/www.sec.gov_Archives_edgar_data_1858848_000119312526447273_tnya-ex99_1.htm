Tenaya Therapeutics Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

Received Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) Designation from U.S. FDA for TN-401 Gene Therapy for PKP2-Associated Arrhythmogenic Right Ventricular Cardiomyopathy

 

TN-201 and TN-401 Program Updates on Upcoming Data Readouts and Regulatory Interactions for Pivotal Trial Planning Anticipated by Year-End

 

Current Cash Position Provides Planned Runway into Fourth Quarter 2027

 

SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., November 4, 2026 – Tenaya Therapeutics, Inc. (NASDAQ: TNYA), a clinical-stage biotechnology company with a mission to discover, develop and deliver potentially curative therapies that address the underlying causes of heart disease, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a corporate update.

 

“As we celebrate the tenth anniversary of Tenaya, we are entering one of the most important periods in the Company’s history,” said Faraz Ali, Chief Executive Officer of Tenaya. “The forthcoming data updates from TN-201 and TN-401, together with regulatory feedback on potential pivotal development pathways, are expected to inform potential opportunities to advance these programs toward late-stage development. We are also preparing to share our vision for TN-301’s potential and future clinical development. Collectively, these milestones are expected to provide important clarity on the path forward for our portfolio and capital allocation decisions, defining the next phase of growth for Tenaya.”

 

Business and Program Updates

 

TN-201 – Gene Therapy for MYBPC3-Associated Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM)

•
Tenaya plans to report longer-term follow-up data from the MyPEAK™-1 clinical trial by year end, including one-year data for Cohort 2 and durability data for Cohort 1 at two years.
•
Tenaya is in ongoing discussions with regulatory authorities with an additional interaction planned to explore late-stage pivotal trial plans for TN-201 and intends to provide an update by year end.

 

TN-401 – Gene Therapy for PKP2-Associated Arrhythmogenic Right Ventricular Cardiomyopathy (ARVC)

•
In September 2026, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) granted Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy (RMAT) designation to TN-401 for the treatment of PKP2-associated ARVC. The RMAT designation was supported by the positive interim data from the ongoing RIDGE™-1 Phase 1b/2 clinical trial.
•
Tenaya expects to report additional interim data from the RIDGE-1 clinical trial by year end, including one-year data for Cohort 2 and durability data for Cohort 1.
•
Tenaya management has been engaging with regulators and has upcoming meetings scheduled to discuss late-stage pivotal trial planning for TN-401. The company plans to provide an update on the feedback received from its discussions, along with the additional TN-401 data, by year end.

 

TN-301 – Small Molecule HDAC6 Inhibitor for the Potential Treatment of Heart Failure with Preserved Ejection Fraction (HFpEF) and Related Cardiac, Metabolic, or Muscular Diseases

•
Tenaya is currently conducting study-enabling work to support the advancement of TN-301 toward Phase 2 clinical trials in patients. The company intends to initiate at least one company-sponsored Phase 2 clinical trial in the second half of 2027.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

•
Cash: As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents were $66.8 million. Tenaya expects that such resources will be sufficient to fund planned operations into the fourth quarter of 2027.
•
Research & Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $14.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $15.4 million for the same period in 2025. Non-cash stock-based compensation included in R&D expense was $0.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.5 million for the same period in 2025.
•
General & Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $5.6 million for each of the third quarters of 2026 and 2025. Non-cash stock-based compensation included in G&A expense was $0.9 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $1.4 million for the same period in 2025.
•
Net Loss: Net loss was $18.9 million, or $0.08 loss per share, for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, compared to a net loss of $20.3 million, or $0.12 per share, for the same period in 2025.

 

About Tenaya Therapeutics

Tenaya Therapeutics is a clinical-stage biotechnology company committed to a bold mission: to discover, develop and deliver potentially curative therapies that address the underlying drivers of heart disease. Tenaya’s pipeline includes clinical-stage candidates TN-201, a gene therapy for MYBPC3-associated hypertrophic cardiomyopathy (HCM); TN-401, a gene therapy for PKP2-associated arrhythmogenic right ventricular cardiomyopathy (ARVC); and TN-301, a highly specific small molecule HDAC6 inhibitor with broad potential clinical utility in cardiac, metabolic and muscular conditions, including heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (HFpEF) and Duchenne muscular dystrophy (DMD). Tenaya has employed a suite of integrated internal capabilities including modality agnostic target discovery and validation, to generate a portfolio of novel medicines based on genetic insights, aimed at the treatment of both rare genetic disorders and more prevalent heart conditions. For more information, visit www.tenayatherapeutics.com.
