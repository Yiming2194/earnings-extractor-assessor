PART III - NARRATIVE
State below in reasonable detail why Forms 10-K, 20-F, 11-K, 10-Q, 10-D, N-CEN, N-CSR, or the transition report
or portion thereof, could not be filed within the prescribed time period.
GoPro, Inc., a Delaware corporation (the “Company”), is unable to file its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for its
fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026 (the “Q3 Form 10-Q”), within the time period prescribed for such report
without unreasonable effort or expense. The Company requires additional time to complete its financial close
procedures and documentation for the accounting and valuation of certain financing arrangements entered into
during the third quarter of 2026. As previously disclosed in Part II, Item 9A of the Company’s Annual Report on
Form 10-K/A for the year ended December 31, 2025, and most recently in Part I, Item 4 of the Company’s Quarterly
Report on Form 10-Q/A for the quarter ended June 30, 2026, each filed with the Securities and Exchange
Commission (the "SEC") on October 19, 2026, the Company identified a material weakness in its internal control
over financial reporting because it did not design and maintain effective controls to evaluate the accounting for and
valuation of embedded features within financing arrangements, including controls to evaluate the inputs and
assumptions used in the valuation, which material weakness has not been remediated. In addition, the Company is
actively seeking waivers from its lenders and evaluating the financial reporting and disclosure implications of
noncompliance with certain covenants under its financing arrangements if waivers are not received. If such waivers
are not obtained, the noncompliance, which constitutes an event of default under such financing arrangements and,
through cross-default provisions, under certain of the Company’s other debt agreements, could permit the lenders to
exercise remedies, including accelerating the repayment of the related indebtedness. Any such acceleration could
result in the failure to meet a closing condition of the Company’s pending merger and permit the acquirer to
terminate the merger agreement.
For the same reasons, the Company is unable to file its Form 8-K announcing the Company’s third quarter 2026
earnings (the “Q3 Earnings 8-K”). The Company continues to work diligently to complete these procedures and
expects to file its Q3 Form 10-Q and Q3 Earnings 8-K as soon as practicable.

The Company’s results of operations for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 will reflect significant changes from
the quarter ended September 30, 2025. While the Company is still completing its third quarter 2026 financial close
process, it is able to provide certain preliminary results. The Company expects to report the following for the quarter
ended September 30, 2026: (i) net revenue of approximately $96 million, as compared to $163 million for the same
period in 2025; (ii) gross margin of approximately 12.7%, as compared to 35.1% for the same period in 2025; and
(iii) operating expenses of approximately $60 million, as compared to $73 million for the same period in 2025. The
Company’s financial statements for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 will continue to reflect there is substantial
doubt about the Company’s ability to continue as a going concern for the next twelve months.
These are preliminary results based on current expectations and are still under review and subject to change. Actual
results may differ.
