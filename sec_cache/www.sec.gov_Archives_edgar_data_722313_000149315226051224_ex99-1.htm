Nortech Systems Reports Third Quarter Results

 

MINNEAPOLIS – November 12, 2026 – Nortech Systems Incorporated (Nasdaq: NSYS) (“Nortech” or the “Company”), a leading provider of engineering and manufacturing solutions for complex electromedical and electromechanical products serving the medical imaging, medical device, industrial, and aerospace & defense markets, reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

2026 Q3 Highlights:

 

  ● Net sales of $33.0 million in Q3 2026 vs. $30.5 million in Q3 2025
  ● Net income of $637 thousand, or $0.21 per diluted share in Q3 2026 vs. net loss of $(146) thousand, or $(0.05) per diluted share in Q3 2025
  ● Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”) of $2.0 million in Q3 2026 vs. $1.3 million in Q3 2025
  ● 90-day backlog of $31.5 million as of September 30, 2026 vs. $31.3 million as of September 30, 2025
  ● Total backlog of $92.8 million as of September 30, 2026, up 20% from September 30, 2025

 

Management Commentary

 

“Our third quarter results reflect continued execution of our business strategy,” said President & CEO, Jay D. Miller. “We are deepening engineering partnerships with our customers, using our manufacturing footprint across the United States, Mexico, and China to serve them from the location that best meets their needs, and strengthening operational discipline across our global operations. These efforts delivered year-over-year revenue growth and improved gross margin, and they are building momentum with our customers.”

 

“That momentum is reflected in our backlog. Total backlog of $92.8 million is up 20% from a year ago, led by Aerospace & Defense and Industrial markets, and gives us confidence in the direction of the business. Our China operations, where net sales grew more than 56% in the quarter, continue to demonstrate the value of our global footprint. As we close out 2026, we are focused on converting backlog into profitable shipments, strengthening cash generation through disciplined purchasing and inventory management, and advancing our fiber optic technologies to support our customers’ next-generation applications.”

About Nortech Systems Incorporated

 

Nortech Systems is a leading provider of design and manufacturing solutions for complex electromedical devices, electromechanical systems, assemblies, and components. Nortech primarily serves the medical imaging, medical device, aerospace & defense, and industrial markets. Its design services span concept development to commercial design, and include medical device, software, electrical, mechanical, and biomedical engineering. Its manufacturing and supply chain capabilities are vertically integrated around wire, cable, and interconnect assemblies, printed circuit board assemblies, as well as system-level assembly, integration, and final test. Headquartered in Maple Grove, Minn., Nortech currently has six manufacturing locations and design centers across the U.S., Latin America, and Asia. Nortech Systems is traded on the NASDAQ Stock Market under the symbol NSYS. Nortech’s website is www.nortechsys.com.

Reconciliation of Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (“GAAP”) Measures to Non-GAAP Financial Measure

 

EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure used by management that we believe provides useful information to investors because it reflects ongoing performance excluding certain non-recurring items during comparable periods and facilitates comparisons between peer companies since interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization can differ greatly between different organizations as a result of differing capital structures and tax strategies. EBITDA is defined as net income (loss) plus interest expense, plus income tax expense plus depreciation expense and amortization expense. EBITDA should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA reflects the impact of restructuring, of costs to review long-term strategy to evaluate opportunities to enhance shareholder value, and other non-recurring items. These strategy costs totaled $236 thousand in the third quarter of 2026 and $440 thousand for the nine months ended September 30, 2026. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA are not a measurement of our financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered an alternative to net sales or net income (loss), as applicable, or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP and may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures of other businesses. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA have limitations as an analytical metric, and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of our operating results as reported under GAAP.
