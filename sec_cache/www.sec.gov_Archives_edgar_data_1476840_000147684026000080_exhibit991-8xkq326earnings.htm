EXPENSIFY ANNOUNCES Q3 2026 RESULTS
Revenue from New Expensify increased 30% from Q2, and is up 212% y/y.
SAN FRANCISCO, CAL.--(BUSINESS WIRE)--November 5, 2026-- Expensify, Inc. (Nasdaq: EXFY), the easiest way to manage expenses, corporate cards, and travel, today released a letter to shareholders from Founder and CEO David Barrett alongside results for its quarter ended September 30, 2026.
A Message From Our Founder
Another strong quarter, both in financial results, as well as product functionality shipped:
•Cash flow remains strong. Q3 produced $4.0 million in Operating Cash Flow, up 510% from last year, and $3.3 million in Free Cash Flow, up 170% from last year. Some of this was used to repurchase 722,121 shares of Class A common stock, returning approximately $1.3 million to shareholders. Based on this continued strength we've increased FY 2026 FCF guidance by $1.5 million to $13.5 million - $15.5 million.
•New Expensify continues to grow and improve. Revenue from New Expensify increased 30% from Q2, and is up 212% y/y. This growth comes in part from major feature launches, such as dozens of new GL and HR integrations, as well as quality-of-life crowd pleasers like enhanced admin roles and bulk workspace management. But much of it comes from paying particular attention to micro-optimizations along the conversion pipeline, which has increased cohort conversion by 67% y/y.
•Expensify Card continues to expand. We now issue in the UK and 12 EU countries for GBP and EUR bank accounts, with enhanced PIN/biometric controls, one-time/date-bound virtual cards, and more real-time spend controls by currency, max amount, and merchant.
•Expensify Travel economics continue to improve. Our new consolidated travel billing feature enables customers to in effect finance their travel bookings to receive a single monthly debit from their bank account, resulting in substantially more revenue to Expensify from each travel booking.
•Explosive adoption of AI features. New MCP connections doubled last month, and are still accelerating. This is strong everywhere, but especially in Claude, in part due Expensify to being selected for the Claude for Small Business bundle. Our custom agent beta has been adopted by thousands of customers, and is able to handle over 96%1 of real world requests received — out of an infinite range of potential requests.
As we outlined last quarter, Expensify Classic is a stable cash-flow engine whose customer base naturally declines over time, while New Expensify is our growth engine. That growth engine is taking on a massive, largely untapped market. As we enter the final stretch of 2026, I'm extremely proud of what we accomplished, and cannot wait to reveal what we've got in store for 2027.
-david
Founder and CEO of Expensify
1 Represents the portion of individual requests that were completed as requested or rejected with clear, actionable steps for the user to complete the request themselves, based on a random sampling of all requests made between October 26, 2026 and November 1, 2026

Financial
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Revenue, net was $33.0 million, a decrease of 6% as compared to the same period last year.
•Generated $4.0 million of cash from operating activities.
•Free cash flow was $3.3 million.
•Net loss was $3.3 million, compared to $2.3 million for the same period last year.
•Non-GAAP net income was $3.1 million.
•Adjusted EBITDA was $6.1 million.
•Interchange revenue derived from the Expensify Card grew to $5.8 million, an increase of 8% as compared to the same period last year.
•See Financial Outlook section for free cash flow guidance for fiscal year ending December 31, 2026.
Business
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Paid members - Paid members were 630,000, a decrease of 2% as compared to the same period last year.
•Referral agreement with Visa - Entered into a referral agreement with Visa, under which Visa can refer eligible clients and corporate card programs to Expensify for expense management.
•Expensify Travel accelerates - Trips booked through Expensify Travel grew 40% year over year and 13% from Q2, with Q3 2026 the highest quarter on record, and launched consolidated travel billing so companies can pay for all business travel on a single invoice.
•Expensify Card expands to Europe - Launched the Expensify Card across 13 European markets, including the UK.
•Expanded integrations - Launched native integrations with AI-native ERPs Rillet and DualEntry, a Claude for Small Business connector, and new Workday, HiBob, BambooHR and Certinia integrations.
•Capital return to shareholders - The Company repurchased 722,121 shares of its Class A common stock, totaling approximately $1.3 million.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

We believe our non-GAAP financial measures are useful in evaluating our business, measuring our performance, identifying trends affecting our business, formulating business plans and making strategic decisions. These non-GAAP financial measures are presented for supplemental informational purposes only, should not be considered a substitute for financial information presented in accordance with GAAP, and may be different from similarly titled metrics or measures presented by other companies. Non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for financial information presented under GAAP. There are a number of limitations related to the use of non-GAAP financial measures versus comparable financial measures determined under GAAP. For example, other companies in our industry may calculate these non-GAAP financial measures differently or may use other measures to evaluate their performance. All of these limitations could reduce the usefulness of these non-GAAP financial measures as analytical tools. Investors are encouraged to review the related GAAP financial measures and the reconciliations of these non-GAAP financial measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures and to not rely on any single financial measure to evaluate our business. A reconciliation of each non-GAAP financial measure to the most directly comparable financial measure stated in accordance with GAAP is at the end of this press release.
Adjusted EBITDA. We define adjusted EBITDA as net loss excluding provision for income taxes, other income, net, depreciation and amortization, and stock-based compensation expense.
Non-GAAP net income. We define non-GAAP net income as net loss excluding stock-based compensation expense.
Free cash flow. We define free cash flow as net cash provided by (used in) operating activities excluding changes in settlement assets, net and settlement liabilities, reduced by the purchases of property and equipment and other assets and software development costs.
The tables at the end of the Condensed Consolidated Financial Statements provide reconciliations to the most directly comparable GAAP financial measure to each of these non-GAAP financial measures.
