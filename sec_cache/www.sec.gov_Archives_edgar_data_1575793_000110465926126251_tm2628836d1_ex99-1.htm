Energous Wireless Power Solutions Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

– Third Quarter Revenue Increases 167% Year over Year, Continuing Streak of Consecutive Quarterly Growth

–Year-to-Date Revenue Exceeds Full-Year 2025 Results

– Conference Call Today at 4:30 p.m. Eastern Time

 

SAN JOSE, CA – November 12, 2026 – Energous Corporation d/b/a Energous Wireless Power Solutions (Nasdaq: WATT) (“Energous,” the “Company,” “we,” or “our”), a pioneer in scalable, over-the-air wireless power networks, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, reporting revenue of approximately $3.4 million, more than doubling revenue reported for the same prior year period. Revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $9.6 million, a 270% increase over the same prior year period, and exceeding the revenue recorded for the full year of 2025. The Company also provided an update on recent events and Company highlights.

 

“Our third quarter results reflect continued momentum in the commercial adoption of Energous’ technology, with revenue growing significantly year over year and our year-to-date revenue performance already surpassing full-year 2025.” said Mallorie Burak, CEO and CFO of Energous. “We also made meaningful progress in improving gross margins as we continue to scale production to better support customer demand. Looking ahead, we see significant opportunity to build on this momentum as enterprises increasingly recognize the value of continuous, real-time visibility into their operations. By enabling businesses to better understand where their assets are, how they are moving, and what is happening across their environments, we are building the foundation for more intelligent, data-driven decision-making and automation.”

  

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

  § Revenue for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $3.4 million and $9.6 million, respectively, versus approximately $1.3 million and $2.6 million, respectively, in the same periods in 2025, a 167% and 270% improvement over the same prior year periods. Third quarter 2026 revenue improved 10% over the second quarter of 2026. Year to date 2026 revenue through September 30 exceeded our full year’s revenue reported for 2025 of $5.6 million, representing a 70% improvement over the prior full year results through the first three quarters of 2026 and nearing a third consecutive year of year-over-year revenue growth.
     
  § For the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, gross profit was $0.5 million and $1.7 million, respectively, representing a 5% and 88% increase versus the same prior year periods. Gross margins were 14% and 17%, respectively, for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, reflecting near-term costs associated with ramping production of updated products to fulfill customer demand.

 

  § Gross margin improved from 3% in the second quarter of 2026 to 14% for the third quarter of 2026.  As our offshore contract manufacturer ramps production, we expect gross margins will continue to show improvement in the fourth quarter and into 2027.
     
  § The Company has maintained its quality performance record, with zero product returns for over two years since commercial production of its PowerBridge Pro began in 2024. Ensuring the highest level of product quality remains a key priority for the Company as we work toward widespread adoption of our technology.

 

  § GAAP operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $3.2 million versus $2.6 million for the same period in 2025.

 

  § GAAP net loss and GAAP loss per share were approximately $2.5 million, or $0.45 per basic and diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, versus the net loss and loss per share of approximately $2.1 million, or $1.31 per basic and diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.

 

 

 

 

  § Non-GAAP operating expenses1 for the third quarter of 2026 were approximately $2.9 million, increasing from $2.6 million in the same prior year period.

 

  § Non-GAAP net loss1 was approximately $2.1 million for the third quarter of 2026 versus non-GAAP net loss of approximately $2.0 million for the same prior year period.  For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the non-GAAP net loss was $6.5 million versus $7.1 million during the first nine months of 2025.

 

  § Approximately $28.8 million in cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026.

 

Company Highlights and Updates

 

    Scale — Since the PowerBridge transmitter began shipping in 2024 through September 30, 2026, Energous has shipped over 69,500 transmitters, primarily driven by demand for the 2W PowerBridge Pro.  The pronounced increase in quarterly shipments beginning in the fourth quarter of 2025 demonstrates the shift from supporting market validation to commercial adoption of the Company’s wireless power network solutions for Ambient IoT.
     
   

Regulatory Certification — On July 29, 2026, we announced that we had received FCC certification for the PowerBridge Pro+, advancing commercialization of our end-to-end wireless power solution. The PowerBridge Pro+ combines high-power RF wireless power delivery with integrated BLE gateway functionality in a single infrastructure device, delivering over-the-air power to compatible ambient IoT devices, such as our patented e-Sense battery-free sensor. Integrated with the Energous e-Compass SaaS platform, it enables centralized monitoring, analytics, configuration, and control across enterprise IoT deployments. This significant milestone allows for commercialization of Energous’ end-to-end solution. The press release can be viewed here.

 

Enterprise Adoption and Market Expansion — Energous continues to advance enterprise opportunities across retail logistics, manufacturing, and cold-chain applications. The Company's strategic focus is on converting existing deployments and pilot programs into broader enterprise rollouts, while expanding use cases around real-time inventory visibility, asset tracking, and condition monitoring.

 

Next-Generation Sensing Architecture — Energous is advancing its next-generation e-Sense roadmap through ultra-low-power semiconductor technologies and strategic ecosystem partnerships, with the objective of improving battery-free sensor capabilities, economics, and deployment scalability.

 

e-Compass and Physical Data Intelligence — Energous is expanding its e-Compass platform toward AI-assisted infrastructure management, enterprise APIs, real-time event processing, and trusted physical-data services. These initiatives support the Company's longer-term objective of becoming a foundational infrastructure provider for enterprise Physical AI applications.

 

Strategic Partnerships and Recurring Revenue — Energous is pursuing deeper integration with enterprise IoT and supply chain software ecosystems, while evaluating additional recurring revenue opportunities through infrastructure management, data services, and advanced analytics.

About Energous Wireless Power Solutions

 

Energous Corporation d/b/a Energous Wireless Power Solutions (NASDAQ: WATT) is pioneering scalable, over-the-air wireless power networks that enable unprecedented levels of visibility, control, and intelligent business automation. The Company’s wireless power transmitter and receiver technologies deliver continuous access to wireless power, helping drive a new generation of battery-free devices for asset and inventory tracking and management—from retail sensors, electronic shelf labels, and asset trackers to air quality monitors, motion detectors, and more. For more information, visit http://www.energous.com/ or follow on LinkedIn.
