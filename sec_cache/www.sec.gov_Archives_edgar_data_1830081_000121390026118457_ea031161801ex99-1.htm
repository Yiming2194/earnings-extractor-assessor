RUM Group Inc. Reports Record Third Quarter 2026 Results

 

~ Third Quarter Revenue of $96.6 Million, Up 290% YoY ~

 

~ First Profitable Quarter in the Company’s History on an Adjusted EBITDA Basis ~

 

~ 22,000 GPUs at Utilization of Approximately 85% ~

 

LONGBOAT KEY, Fla., November 11, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) – RUM Group Inc. (Nasdaq: RUM) (“RUM Group” or the “Company”), an AI infrastructure and video company on a mission to unlock the power of human imagination, today announced financial results for the fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Q3 2026 Key Highlights and Key Items

 

●Record quarterly revenue of $96.6 million, up 139% QoQ and 290% YoY, and 3.8% above the top end of our guidance.

 

●Quake AI contributed $68.2 million, or more than 70% of total revenue, in its first full quarter.

 

●The first profitable quarter in the Company’s history on an Adjusted EBITDA basis.

 

●Quake AI continues to deliver strong performance and demand for its existing fleet of approximately 22,000 NVIDIA GPUs, with utilization of approximately 85% for the quarter.

 

●Acquired an additional 8.26 million shares of Northern Data AG (“Northern Data”), increasing ownership from 85.2% to approximately 98%.

 

●Commenced squeeze-out proceedings under the German Stock Corporation Act, expecting to reach 100% ownership of Northern Data in early 2027.

 

●Due to market conditions, a certain portion of our previously disclosed 250MW of unmonetized capacity targeted for 2027 may be pushed out beyond 2027.

 

●Announced a long-term distribution and content partnership with WWE to become the new home of the weekly premium program WWE Main Event.

 

●In the third quarter, began a cost reset in video, tied to a refocused strategy. Expecting to start seeing related savings through 2027.

 

Management Commentary

 

Chris Pavlovski, CEO of RUM Group, commented, “Q3 was a defining quarter for RUM Group. We delivered record revenue, nearly four times last year’s, and achieved positive Adjusted EBITDA for the first time in our history. Quake AI is now the engine of our business. We have real assets running real AI workloads for real customers and generating real cash. Our 22,000-GPU estate is nearly sold out at 85% utilization. As capital becomes more selective, we believe it will flow to experienced operators with disciplined balance sheets. That is exactly where RUM Group stands, and we intend to use that position to build lasting value for our shareholders.”

Revenues increased by $71.8 million to $96.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, reflecting higher Rumble revenues and the contribution of Quake AI. Rumble revenues increased by $3.6 million, driven by a $5.6 million increase in advertising revenue, partially offset by a $2.0 million decrease in other services revenue. The decrease in other services revenue reflected a $1.2 million decrease in subscription revenue and a $0.8 million decrease in licensing, platform hosting fees and other revenues. Quake AI contributed revenues of $68.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to $0 for the three months ended September 30, 2025, reflecting the inclusion of a first full quarter of combined results following the closing of the Northern Data acquisition on June 17, 2026. Quake AI revenues were generated from GPU cloud computing services, provided on a reserved or on-demand basis, as well as data center hosting and colocation services.

 

 

 

 

Cost of services increased by $32.0 million to $57.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was attributable to $30.0 million of Quake AI data center costs and $3.1 million of higher programming and content costs, partially offset by a $1.1 million decrease in other cost of services.

 

General and administrative expenses increased by $15.4 million to $25.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was driven by the acquisition of Northern Data, which contributed $11.0 million of payroll and related expenses and other administrative costs. Excluding Northern Data, the remaining variance reflects a $3.0 million increase in professional fees, a $0.8 million increase in payroll and related expenses and a $0.6 million increase in other administrative costs.

 

Research and development expenses increased by $3.3 million to $7.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was due to an increase in payroll and related expenses of $1.8 million, of which $0.8 million was attributable to Northern Data, and higher costs associated with computer software, hardware, and other expenditures used in research and development-related activities of $1.5 million.

 

Sales and marketing expenses increased by $2.6 million to $7.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was attributable to $2.0 million of higher payroll and related expenses, including $0.7 million from Northern Data, $0.4 million in higher marketing and public relations spending, and $0.2 million in other sales and marketing-related expenditures.

 

As of September 30, 2026, RUM Group had total liquidity of $142.3 million, consisting of $134.4 million in cash and cash equivalents and 94.68 Bitcoin, valued at $7.9 million.

About RUM Group Inc.

 

RUM Group Inc. is an AI infrastructure and video company. Its Quake AI business delivers AI compute as a service, operating AI data centers including GPU and CPU compute, storage, and networking at scale. Rumble, RUM Group’s video business and the original tenant of Quake AI, provides creators and enterprises a full suite of video technologies, unlocking reach, scale, and monetization. Non-U.S. GAAP Financial Measures

 

To supplement our consolidated financial statements, which are prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP, we use certain non-GAAP financial measures, as described below, to understand and evaluate our core operating performance. These non-GAAP financial measures, which may be different than similarly titled measures used by other companies, are presented to enhance investors’ overall understanding of our financial performance and should not be considered a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP. We use the non-GAAP financial measure of Adjusted EBITDA, which is defined as net income (loss) excluding interest income (expense), net, other income (expense), net, provision for income taxes, depreciation and amortization, share-based compensation expense, acquisition-related transaction costs, change in fair value of warrants, change in fair value of digital assets, and change in the fair value of derivative. The Company’s management believes that it is important to consider Adjusted EBITDA, in addition to net income (loss), as it helps identify trends in our business that could otherwise be masked by the effect of the gains and losses that are included in net income (loss) but excluded from Adjusted EBITDA.

 

Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information prepared in accordance with GAAP. There are several limitations related to the use of Adjusted EBITDA rather than net income (loss), the nearest GAAP equivalent. As a result of these limitations, you should consider Adjusted EBITDA alongside other financial performance measures, including net income (loss) and our other financial results presented in accordance with GAAP.
