Consolidated Results of Operations, As Reported and As Adjusted – Three-month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025:

KING OF PRUSSIA, PA – Universal Health Services, Inc. (NYSE: UHS) announced today that its reported net income attributable to UHS was $308.7 million, or $5.22 per diluted share, during the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $373.0 million, or $5.86 per diluted share, during the third quarter of 2025. Net revenues increased by 5.3% to $4.733 billion during the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $4.495 billion during the third quarter of 2025.

Included in our operating results during the third quarter of 2026, was an aggregate net favorable pre-tax impact of approximately $21 million recorded in connection with the following items which were not included in our 2026 operating results forecast, as previously disclosed on July 27, 2026: (i) a favorable net pre-tax impact of $47 million (net of related provider taxes) recorded in connection with increases in amounts received from Medicaid supplemental payment programs in various states (consisting primarily of Washington, D.C. and California), partially offset by; (ii) an unfavorable pre-tax impact of $26 million resulting from an increase to our reserves for self-insured professional and general liability claims due primarily to the tentative settlement agreement reached during the third quarter of 2026 in the matter discussed below in Legal Proceedings Update.

Included in our operating results during the third quarter of 2025, was an aggregate net favorable pre-tax impact of approximately $55 million recorded in connection with the following: (i) $90 million of pre-tax reimbursements (net of related provider taxes), covering the period of October 1, 2024 through September 30, 2025, in connection with a Medicaid state directed payment program in Washington, D.C., and; (ii) a $35 million pre-tax charge incurred to increase our reserves for self-insured professional and general liabilities resulting from unfavorable claims’ trends.

As reflected on the Schedule of Non-GAAP Supplemental Information (“Supplemental Schedule”), there were no adjustments applicable to our operating results during the third quarter of 2026.

As reflected on the Supplemental Schedule, included in our reported results during the third quarter of 2025 were: (i) an unrealized after-tax gain of $10.0 million, or $.16 per diluted share ($13.1 million pre-tax), resulting from an increase in the market value of certain equity securities that were sold during the fourth quarter of 2025 (included in “Other (income) expense, net”), and; (ii) a favorable net after-tax impact of $0.6 million, or $.01 per diluted share, resulting from the net tax benefit recorded in connection with “ASU 2016-09”, Compensation – Stock Compensation: Improvements to Employee Share-Based Payment Accounting, net of the impact of executive compensation limitations pursuant to IRC section 162(m). After giving effect to these items, our adjusted net income during the third quarter of 2025 was $362.3 million, or $5.69 per diluted share.

As calculated on the attached Supplemental Schedule, our earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation & amortization (“EBITDA net of NCI”, NCI is net income attributable to noncontrolling interests), was $640.4 million during the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $684.2 million during the third quarter of 2025. Our adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation & amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA net of NCI”), which

excludes the impact of other (income) expense, net, was $624.6 million during the third quarter of 2026, as compared to $670.6 million during the third quarter of 2025.

Consolidated Results of Operations, As Reported and As Adjusted – Nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025:

Reported net income attributable to UHS was $1.016 billion, or $16.86 per diluted share, during the first nine months of 2026, as compared to $1.043 billion, or $16.07 per diluted share, during the comparable period of 2025. Net revenues increased by 7.7% to $13.867 billion during the first nine months of 2026, as compared to $12.879 billion during the comparable period of 2025.

As reflected on the Supplemental Schedule, our adjusted net income during the first nine months of 2026 was $1.014 billion, or $16.83 per diluted share, as compared to $1.030 billion, or $15.87 per diluted share, during the comparable period of 2025.

As reflected on the Supplemental Schedule, included in our reported results during the first nine months of 2026 was a favorable net after-tax impact of $2.2 million, or $.03 per diluted share, resulting from the net tax benefit recorded in connection with ASU 2016-09. Included in our reported results during the first nine months of 2025 were the following: (i) an unrealized after-tax gain of $11.3 million, or $.17 per diluted share ($14.7 million pre-tax), resulting from an increase in the market value of certain equity securities that were sold during the fourth quarter of 2025, and; (ii) a favorable net after-tax impact of $1.9 million, or $.03 per diluted share, resulting from the net tax benefit recorded in connection with ASU 2016-09.

As calculated on the attached Supplemental Schedule, our EBITDA net of NCI, was $1.972 billion during the first nine months of 2026, as compared to $1.939 billion during the comparable period of 2025. Our Adjusted EBITDA net of NCI, which excludes the impact of other (income) expense, net, was $1.951 billion during the first nine months of 2026, as compared to $1.912 billion during the comparable period of 2025.

Acute Care Services – Three and nine-month periods ended September 30, 2026 and 2025:

During the third quarter of 2026, at our acute care hospitals owned during both periods (“same facility basis”), adjusted admissions (adjusted for outpatient activity) and adjusted patient days each increased by 4.9%, as compared to the third quarter of 2025. At these facilities, during the third quarter of 2026, net revenue per adjusted admission and net revenue per adjusted patient day each decreased by 1.7%, as compared to the third quarter of 2025. Net revenues generated from our acute care services, on a same facility basis, increased by 4.7% during the third quarter of 2026, as compared to the third quarter of 2025.

During the first nine months of 2026, on a same facility basis, adjusted admissions increased by 2.6% and adjusted patient days increased by 2.9%, as compared to the comparable period of 2025. Legal Proceedings Update:

The disclosure below provides an update for recent developments related to the matter referenced which has been previously disclosed in various Forms 10-Q and 10-K, as filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission, most recently our Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended June 30, 2026.

K.E.E., et al., Plaintiffs v. Cumberland Hospital, LLC d/b/a Cumberland Hospital for Children and Adolescents, et al. (and related lawsuits)

Cumberland Hospital for Children and Adolescents (“Cumberland”), an indirect subsidiary of Universal Health Services, Inc. (the “Company”) and UHS of Delaware, Inc. (“UHS Delaware”), our administrative services company, are defendants in multi-plaintiff lawsuits filed in the Circuit Court for Richmond, Virginia (the “Cumberland Litigation”). The lawsuits relate to allegations of, among other things, inappropriate sexual contact during medical examinations by Dr. Daniel Davidow, an independent contractor and the former medical director for Cumberland.

The claims asserted by three of the plaintiffs in the Cumberland Litigation were consolidated for trial in September 2024. There are approximately 53 additional claimants making similar allegations with claims pending in the Cumberland Litigation. The Company and UHS Delaware were dismissed from the September 2024 trial on the first three plaintiffs’ claims. In late September 2024, a jury entered a verdict finding Dr. Davidow and Cumberland liable and awarded these three plaintiffs combined compensatory damages of $60

million for all liability theories, an additional combined $180 million in trebled damages for violation of the Virginia Consumer Protection Act, and an additional combined $120 million in punitive damages. Based upon Virginia law, the Court reduced the punitive damage amount to a combined maximum of $1.05 million ($350,000 per plaintiff).

In August 2026, we reached a tentative settlement agreement, which is subject to execution of final release agreements and certain court approvals (as necessary), to resolve all of the outstanding claims related to the current claimants in the Cumberland Litigation. Pursuant to the tentative settlement agreement, the terms of the aggregate settlement are confidential. We believe a substantial portion of the tentative aggregate settlement amount, and related expenses, is covered by our commercial excess insurance and the remainder is covered by our reserves recorded in connection with the Cumberland Litigation.
