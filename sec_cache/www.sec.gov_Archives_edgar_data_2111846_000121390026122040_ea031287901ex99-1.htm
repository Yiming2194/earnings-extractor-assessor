Game Your Game Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Business Highlights

 

Transitioned GameGolf KZN AI™ from limited release to full commercial availability during the quarter

 

Formed Altus Sports Group to Expand into Talent Representation, Athlete Branding and Sports Marketing

 

 

 

PALO ALTO, Calif., Nov. 19, 2026 -- Game Your Game, Inc. (Nasdaq: GYGY) (“Game Your Game” or the “Company”), an AI-powered sports performance technology company, today reported financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

 

The third quarter was a transitional period for the Company. Game Your Game began trading on the Nasdaq Capital Market on July 30, 2026 through a direct listing, completed the transition of its next-generation GameGolf KZN AI™ platform to full commercial availability on September 23, 2026 and formed the Altus Sports Group subsidiary to expand into talent representation, athlete branding and sports marketing. As a result, the quarter reflects the costs of becoming a public company and of preparing for commercial launch, with only a partial period of product sales.

 

“The third quarter was about readying the product to put into the hands of golfers everywhere. We listed on Nasdaq in July, and opened GameGolf KZN AI to everyone in September. The fourth quarter is our first full quarter with GameGolf KZN AI on sale for general availability and will focus our attention on growing our subscriber base directly and through partnerships, affiliates, and coaches,” said Soumya Das, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Game Your Game.

 

Third Quarter 2026 and Other Recent Business Highlights

 

●Completed a direct listing on the Nasdaq Capital Market, with the Company’s common stock beginning trading under the symbol “GYGY” on July 30, 2026.

 

●Announced an initial quarterly purchase of 500 GameGolf KZN AI™ units from GolfSuites, an indoor golf entertainment company.

 

●Began the limited release of GameGolf KZN AI™ to a select group of golfers ahead of general availability.

 

●Formed Altus Sports Group, Inc. (“ASG”), a majority-owned subsidiary that expands the Company’s platform beyond golf technology and into talent representation, athlete branding and sports marketing. The Company owns 70% of ASG, with the remaining 30% held by ASG’s four founders and subject to vesting.

 

●Completed the transition of GameGolf KZN AI™ from limited release to full commercial availability, with the platform available for purchase at gamegolf.com.

 

●Announced the GameGolf Affiliate Program, a commission-based program that partners with golf creators, coaches, teaching professionals, club fitters and golf communities to introduce GameGolf KZN AI™ to more golfers.

 

●Launched the “Explore Golf Course” feature, the first new feature introduced since full commercial availability, giving golfers hole-by-hole insight into the Company’s database of more than 36,000 mapped golf courses, course leaderboards and social play.

 

 

 

 

 

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

 

●Revenue: $106,813 for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $25,765 for the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 315%, primarily attributable to sales of GameGolf KZN AI devices to our reseller GolfSuites following the general release and commercial launch of GameGolf KZN AI on September 23, 2026, which occurred during the last week of the quarter. GolfSuites accounted for approximately 98% of third quarter revenue.

 

●Gross margin: 37% for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 9% for the third quarter of 2025, on gross profit of $39,298 compared with $2,252. The improvement reflects device sales at reseller pricing to GolfSuites rather than the discounted pricing offered to beta program participants.

 

●Operating expenses: $2,938,969 for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $248,697 for the third quarter of 2025, primarily reflecting $1,155,224 of non-cash share-based compensation recognized in the quarter under the Company’s letter agreement with Maxim Group LLC, the award under which was fully recognized as of the quarter ended September 30, 2026, and accounting, legal and other professional costs incurred in connection with the direct listing. Loss from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $2,899,671, compared with $246,445 for the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Net loss: $3,160,790 for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $324,577 for the third quarter of 2025. Net loss attributable to common stockholders was $5,236,136, or $0.37 per basic and diluted share, after $2,104,687 of primarily non-cash Series A Preferred Stock dividends, including a $1,444,444 increase in the Stated Value of the Series A Preferred Stock resulting from a Trigger Event that occurred on September 14, 2026 when the trading price of the common stock declined below specified levels.

 

●Balance sheet: Cash was $4,433,642 at September 30, 2026, compared with $87,463 at December 31, 2025, and working capital was $5,055,027 as of September 30, 2026, compared with a working capital deficit of $2,331,221 at December 31, 2025. Total stockholders’ equity was $2,901,837 as of September 30, 2026, compared with a stockholders’ deficit of $2,297,556 at December 31, 2025. Net cash used in operating activities was $3,858,887 for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared with $748,185 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

Additional information regarding the Company’s financial condition and results of operations for the third quarter of 2026, including information regarding subsequent events and risk factors, is available in the Company’s Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q for the quarterly period ended September 30, 2026, filed with the SEC and available at www.sec.gov and in the Investors section of the Company’s website at investors.gameyourgame.com.

 

About Game Your Game, Inc.

 

Game Your Game, Inc. (Nasdaq: GYGY) is an AI-powered sports performance technology company. The Company develops and markets the GameGolf KZN AI™ platform — an integrated golf performance ecosystem of proprietary shot-tracking hardware and subscription-based software solutions. The platform leverages advanced GPS tracking, embedded neural network technology, and AI-powered analytics to provide golfers of all skill levels with real-time insights, on-course strategy recommendations, and personalized performance data. Game Your Game’s technology has been adopted by golfers in more than 140 countries, with over 36,000 golf courses mapped and an estimated 300 million shots tracked across the lifetime of its platform. The Company is headquartered in Palo Alto, California. For more information, visit www.gameyourgame.com. Altus Sports Group, Inc., a majority-owned subsidiary of Game Your Game, Inc., was formed to build a connected platform for the sports business across talent representation, athlete branding and sports marketing. For more information, visit www.altus-sports.com.
