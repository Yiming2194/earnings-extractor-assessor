EXHIBIT 99.1

Northern Technologies International Corporation Reports Financial Results for Fourth Quarter and Full Year Fiscal 2026

Fiscal 2026 Consolidated Net Sales Increased 13.9% Year-Over-Year to a Record $95,982,000, Driven by Growth Across ZERUST® Industrial, ZERUST® Oil and Gas and Natur-Tec®

Fourth Quarter Consolidated Net Sales Up 18.6% Year-Over-Year to a Quarterly Record $26,461,000,
Driven by Strong ZERUST Industrial and Natur-Tec Demand

Company Focused on Accelerating Sales Growth and Improving Operating Profitability

MINNEAPOLIS, Nov. 19, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Northern Technologies International Corporation (NASDAQ: NTIC), a leading developer of corrosion inhibiting products and services, as well as bio-based and certified compostable (fully biodegradable) polymer resin compounds, today reported its financial results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended August 31, 2026.

“Fiscal 2026 was a year of strong global demand, with record annual net sales of approximately $96 million and continued momentum across our core businesses. These results reflect the strength of our product lines, longstanding customer relationships, and established global footprint,” said Gautam Ramdas, President and CEO of NTIC.
“Since becoming CEO in September 2026, I have focused on deepening engagement with our customers, aligning our organization around the growth opportunities ahead, and strengthening collaboration with our global joint venture partners to unlock new avenues for cooperation. Equally important has been reinforcing our technology and innovation capabilities to support our long-term growth ambitions. These early engagements have strengthened my confidence in NTIC’s foundation and our ability to capitalize on the opportunities ahead.”

“As we enter fiscal 2027, we are focused on taking NTIC to the next level by accelerating growth and translating that growth into stronger profitability. A key area of emphasis will be leveraging our proprietary technologies and deep corrosion prevention expertise to deliver innovative, engineered solutions that address increasingly complex customer needs. We also see meaningful opportunities to expand our ZERUST industrial business, drive further growth in ZERUST oil and gas, and broaden adoption of our Natur-Tec solutions. Combined with greater operational discipline and a continued focus on efficiency, we believe these initiatives can support sustainable, profitable growth and create meaningful long-term value for our stockholders. I am excited about the opportunities ahead and look forward to updating stockholders on our progress,” concluded Mr. Ramdas.

NTIC’s consolidated net sales increased 18.6% to $26,461,000 during the three months ended August 31, 2026, compared to $22,315,000 for the three months ended August 31, 2025. The year-over-year increase in fourth quarter sales was primarily driven by increased sales and demand for ZERUST® industrial and Natur-Tec® products, partially offset by lower ZERUST® oil and gas sales. For the full year ended August 31, 2026, consolidated net sales increased 13.9% to $95,982,000, compared to $84,234,000 for the same period last year. NTIC China net sales increased 13.1% to $18,372,000 for fiscal 2026, compared to $16,240,000 for fiscal 2025.

Net sales at NTIC’s joint ventures, which are not consolidated with NTIC’s financial results, increased 4.0% to $25,351,000 during the three months ended August 31, 2026, compared to $24,388,000 for the three months ended August 31, 2025. NTIC’s total income from joint venture operations decreased 27.7% to $1,567,000 during the three months ended August 31, 2026, compared to $2,168,000 during the three months ended August 31, 2025. The $601,000 decrease in total income from joint venture operations was primarily due to lower fees for services provided to joint ventures, following the modification of fees at EXCOR and Acobal, and lower profitability at EXCOR. For the full fiscal year 2026, NTIC’s joint venture operating income was $8,436,000, compared to joint venture operating income of $8,545,000 for the prior fiscal year. Net sales of NTIC’s joint ventures were $100,074,000 for full fiscal year 2026, compared to $91,236,000 for the prior fiscal year, an increase of 9.7%.

Gross profit, as a percentage of net sales, decreased 256 basis points to 35.0% for fiscal 2026, from 37.6% for fiscal 2025. The decrease was primarily due to: (i) higher raw material costs, including resin and solvents, that were only partly recovered through price increases; (ii) lower selling prices on certain Natur-Tec® products; and (iii) a lower-margin sales mix.

Operating expenses, as a percentage of net sales, for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 were 39.0%, compared to 43.5% for the same period last fiscal year. For the full fiscal year 2026, operating expenses, as a percent of net sales, were 41.4%, compared to 44.7% for the same period last fiscal year. Operating expenses for the three and twelve months ended August 31, 2026 increased 6.4% and 5.5%, respectively. These increases were primarily due to strategic investments in ZERUST® oil and gas marketing and sales efforts, including personnel expenses and the corresponding benefits, as well as increased travel and professional fees.

NTIC recognized $1,140,000 in other income during the twelve months ended August 31, 2025, due to the receipt of a cash employee retention credit payment. No other income was recognized during the twelve months ended August 31, 2026.

Income before income tax expense was $1,791,000 for fiscal 2026, compared to $3,031,000 for fiscal 2025. NTIC’s effective income tax rate increased to 86.3% for fiscal year 2026, from 67.5% in the prior fiscal year. The increase primarily reflects lower pre-tax income relative to income taxes incurred at NTIC’s profitable foreign subsidiaries, which are taxed at statutory rates that exceed the U.S. rate in certain jurisdictions (notably Brazil and India) and bear withholding taxes on royalty and dividend payments. NTIC also recorded no income tax benefit on its U.S. pre-tax loss, as a full valuation allowance is maintained against its U.S. deferred tax assets. These items were partially offset by non-taxable equity income from NTIC’s international joint ventures and research and development tax credits. As a result, NTIC’s effective tax rate was unusually high and volatile for fiscal year 2026. Although management expects the effective tax rate to normalize in future periods when additional profits are recognized in North American operations, no assurance can be provided that it will.

Net loss attributable to NTIC for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was $642,000, or $0.07 per diluted share, compared to a net loss attributable to NTIC of $1,100,000, or $0.12 per diluted share, for the same period last fiscal year. For the full fiscal year ended August 31, 2026, net loss attributable to NTIC was $703,000, or $0.07 per diluted share, compared to net income attributable to NTIC of $18,000, or $0.00 per diluted share, for last fiscal year.

NTIC’s non-GAAP adjusted net loss(1), as set forth in the GAAP reconciliation at the end of this release, was $537,000, or $0.06 per diluted share, for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, compared to a net loss of $607,000, or $0.06 per diluted share, for the same quarter last fiscal year. For the fiscal year ended August 31, 2026, non-GAAP adjusted net loss was $280,000, or $0.03 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $12,000, or $0.00 per diluted share, for last fiscal year.

NTIC had working capital of $20,073,000 as of August 31, 2026, including $7,018,000 in cash and cash equivalents and an outstanding revolving line of credit and current portion of term loan balance of $14,161,000, compared to $20,439,000 of working capital as of August 31, 2025, including $7,251,000 in cash and cash equivalents and an outstanding revolving line of credit and current portion of term loan balance of $12,189,000.

During the third quarter of fiscal 2026, the Company committed to a plan to sell its Beachwood, Ohio facility, which has historically been used by the Company’s ZERUST® segment, and reclassified the carrying value of the property, $855,730 as of August 31, 2026, from property, plant and equipment, net, to assets held for sale on the consolidated balance sheet. The purchase and sale agreement entered into following a May 2026 non-binding letter of intent to purchase the property for $1,150,000 in cash was terminated in early September 2026, and the Company resumed marketing the property. On September 10, 2026, the Company signed a new non-binding letter of intent with a different, unrelated buyer to purchase the property for $1,100,000 in cash, and on November 3, 2026 the Company and the buyer entered into a definitive purchase and sale agreement on those terms. The buyer has a 60-day inspection period during which it may terminate the agreement for any reason, and the Company expects the sale of the property to close in the second quarter of fiscal 2027; however, there can be no assurance that the sale will be completed on the terms described or at all.

At August 31, 2026, NTIC had $30,707,000 of investments in joint ventures, of which $18,056,000, or 58.8%, was cash, with the remaining balance mostly made up of other working capital. This is compared to $28,612,000 of investments in joint ventures, of which $14,800,000, or 51.7%, is cash, with the remaining balance mostly made up of other working capital at August 31, 2025.

About Northern Technologies International Corporation

Northern Technologies International Corporation develops and markets proprietary, environmentally beneficial products and services in over 65 countries either directly or via a network of subsidiaries, joint ventures, independent distributors and agents. NTIC’s primary business is corrosion prevention marketed mainly under the ZERUST® brand. NTIC has been selling its proprietary ZERUST® rust and corrosion inhibiting products and services to the automotive, general industrial, mechanical, mining, agricultural, and retail consumer markets for over 50 years and, more recently, has also expanded into the oil and gas industry. NTIC offers worldwide on-site technical consulting for rust and corrosion prevention issues. NTIC’s technical service consultants work directly with the end users of NTIC’s products to analyze their specific needs and develop systems to meet their technical requirements. NTIC also markets and sells a portfolio of proprietary bio-based and certified compostable (fully biodegradable) polymer resin compounds and finished products marketed under the Natur-Tec® brand.
