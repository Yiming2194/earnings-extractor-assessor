MIAMI, FL – November 13, 2026 – Digi Power X Inc. (Nasdaq: DGXX / Cboe Canada: DGX) (“Digi Power X” or the “Company”), an AI data center infrastructure operator, today reported its financial and operating results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026 (all amounts in U.S. dollars, unless otherwise indicated). The Company’s quarterly report on Form 10-Q, which includes unaudited consolidated financial statements and management’s discussion and analysis (“MD&A”) for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, is being filed and made accessible under the Company’s continuous disclosure profile on SEDAR+ at www.sedarplus.ca and will also be available on EDGAR at www.sec.gov/edgar.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

 

All amounts in U.S. dollars (millions)

 

●Revenue of $12.25 million, up 85% from the previously completed fiscal quarter and an increase of 50% compared to the quarter ended September 30, 2025;

 

●$119.6 million in cash, cash equivalents and digital currencies, $103.0 million of working capital and no outstanding borrowings at quarter end;

Columbiana: Contracted Revenue Ahead

 

The Company’s ten-year take-or-pay colocation agreement with Cerebras, its AI colocation tenant at its Columbiana, Alabama campus is expected to provide annual fees for Phase 1 (15 MW) averaging approximately $40 million per year over the ten-year term, for an approximate aggregate total of $402 million. Subject to satisfaction of various conditions, Phase 2 (25 MW) would add approximately $58.5 million in annual fees, bringing combined average annual fees to approximately $107 million per year, and total contract value to approximately $1.07 billion if both phases commence, with an option to expand the relationship for an additional term that could increase the total potential contract value to approximately $2.5 billion.

NeoCloudz Token Factory is Live

 

This quarter, Digi Power X launched the NeoCloudz Token Factory, taking the Company from AI infrastructure provider to direct seller of AI tokens. Running on Digi Power X’s own power, data centers and GPUs, the platform sells AI inference straight to customers. Because the Company owns the full stack, it keeps the value of every token it serves instead of renting out compute at wholesale prices. Digi Power X expects the Token Factory to unlock a new, fast-growing revenue stream with meaningfully higher margins, marking a major step in turning its infrastructure into a full-scale AI business.

 

“Going live with the NeoCloudz Token Factory is a major step for Digi Power X. We own the power, the data centers and the GPUs, and now we’re selling tokens directly to customers. That lets us earn more from every megawatt we operate, and we’re excited to grow this business alongside our existing infrastructure.”

 

— Alec Amar, President, Digi Power X Inc.

 

$200 Million Project-Level Debt Financing

 

As previously announced on October 13, 2026, the Company received a commitment letter from a major lending institution for US$200 million in debt financing, syndicated by Goldman Sachs, to support the development of its 40 MW AI data center in Columbiana, Alabama. The financing is expected to be structured at a project special purpose vehicle level, with an unsecured Company guarantee through project completion and other limited matters. Proceeds are intended to support completion of the Columbiana, Alabama project and related project costs. Closing is subject to definitive documentation and the satisfaction of various conditions, including the lender’s completion of its diligence. There can be no assurance that the financing will be completed on the anticipated terms or at all.

 

2027 Outlook

 

Based on its contracted business, available power and planned AI infrastructure deployments, the Company expects to reach a revenue run-rate of approximately $250 million to $300 million annualized, or approximately $21 million to $25 million per month, by the end of the third quarter of 2027, subject to adequate financing and market and various other conditions. Approximately $143 million of that annualized run-rate (about $12 million per month) is supported by current operations and the Columbiana tenant agreement, including conditional Phase 2. The balance is expected to come from additional AI colocation and GPU capacity currently in development and in customer negotiations.

 

2027 Capital Plan

 

The Company expects 2027 capital expenditures of approximately $350 million, primarily for the build-out of approximately 10 MW of AI inference (“token factory”) capacity and the completion of Phase 2 at Columbiana. The Company intends to fund this program principally through customer deposits and prepayments and debt financing, including the proposed $200 million facility, together with cash on hand. This approach ties capital deployment to contracted revenue and is designed to minimize dilution to shareholders.

US Data Centers: Modular AI Infrastructure

 

Digi Power X owns approximately 48% of US Data Centers Inc. (“USDC”), which designs and deploys ARMS modular AI data center systems. Digi Power X accounts for its investment in USDS under the equity method. USDC is independently raising capital to fund its expansion (which results in further dilution of the interest of Digi Power X), without capital contributions from Digi Power X and without the issuance of Digi Power X shares. USDC is also developing URP-1, a robotics platform for automated deployment and maintenance of modular data center infrastructure.

 

Opportunity for Growth and Expansion

 

Digi Power X continues to prepare its New York infrastructure assets for conversion to AI data center operations, providing opportunity to expand its AI portfolio. The Company is targeting the transition of these sites beginning in Q3 and Q4 2027.

 

The Company is also advancing development planning for a large-scale AI data center campus in North Carolina, where the Company owns approximately 40 acres adjacent to one of North Carolina’s largest electrical switchyards. The current development plan contemplates approximately 75 MW in 2029 and an additional 75 MW in 2030. The Company also continues to assess opportunities associated with its previously announced LOI involving a 1.3 GW power generation asset in West Virginia.

 

Between its New York infrastructure assets, North Carolina development plans and West Virginia power asset, Digi Power X can expand its aggregate power capacity to over 400 MW, through a geographically diversified portfolio of power-secured AI infrastructure, if its development plans are fully realized.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. The Company defines EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest expense, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization. Management believes that providing this non-GAAP financial measure, which excludes items that vary significantly with the Company’s capital structure, tax position and the age and cost of its asset base, allows for meaningful comparisons of the Company’s core business operating results across reporting periods. It also provides the Company with an important tool for financial and operational decision making and for evaluating its own core business operating results over different periods of time. The Company’s EBITDA measure may not be directly comparable to similar measures provided by other companies in our industry, as other companies in our industry may calculate non-GAAP financial results differently. EBITDA does not reflect the Company’s capital expenditures, the cash required to service debt, or income tax payments, and it does not reflect the cost of replacing assets being depreciated and amortized. The Company’s EBITDA is not a measurement of financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as a substitute for, or superior to, net income (loss) or any other measure of performance calculated in accordance with GAAP.

 

Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization, and further adjusted to exclude share-based compensation, digital currency revaluation, gains or losses on sale of digital currencies, foreign exchange gains or losses, changes in fair value of financial instruments (including warrant liabilities), gain/loss on settlement of debt, gains or losses on deconsolidation of subsidiaries, and gains or losses on sale of property and equipment. Management believes that providing this non-GAAP financial measure that excludes these items allows for meaningful comparisons between the Company’s core business operating results and those of other companies and provides the Company with an important tool for financial and operational decision making and for evaluating its own core business operating results over different periods of time. In addition to management’s internal use of non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA, management believes that Adjusted EBITDA is also useful to investors and analysts in comparing our performance across reporting periods on a consistent basis. The Company’s Adjusted EBITDA measure may not be directly comparable to similar measures provided by other companies in our industry, as other companies in our industry may calculate non-GAAP financial results differently. The Company’s Adjusted EBITDA is not a measurement of financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as a substitute for, or superior to, net income (loss) or any other measure of performance calculated in accordance with GAAP.
