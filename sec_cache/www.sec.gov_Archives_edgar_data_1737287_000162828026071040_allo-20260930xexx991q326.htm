SOUTH SAN FRANCISCO, Calif., November 5, 2026 – Allogene Therapeutics, Inc. (Nasdaq: ALLO), a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering the development of allogeneic CAR T (AlloCAR T) products for cancer and autoimmune disease, today provided corporate updates and reported financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“CAR T has transformed what's possible in medicine, but toxicity and treatment complexity have kept its full potential out of reach for too many patients,” said Zachary Roberts, M.D., Ph.D., President and Chief Executive Officer of Allogene. “We believe 2027 will be an important year for cema-cel and the field, with upcoming ALPHA3 data expected to provide important insights into the potential for CAR T to be integrated into existing practice patterns, earlier in the course of a patient’s disease and closer to where they receive care. At the same time, our ALLO-329 data set to be released next week will begin to address an equally important question in autoimmune disease: whether we can deliver meaningful clinical benefit with less reliance on chemotherapy-based conditioning. Both programs reflect our conviction that the future of CAR T will be shaped not only by what these therapies can do, but by how we make them more accessible and practical for patients.”

Cema-Cel: Pivotal ALPHA3 Trial in 1L Consolidation in LBCL
Cemacabtagene ansegedleucel (cema-cel) is being evaluated in ALPHA3, the first pivotal, randomized trial in LBCL designed to assess whether minimal residual disease (MRD)-guided treatment following first-line therapy can delay or prevent clinical relapse.

Enrollment momentum continues, supported by expanded site participation, increasing MRD screening activity, strong investigator engagement, and brisk enrollment in the Asia-Pacific region following the positive interim futility analysis reported earlier this year. The growing ALPHA3 network includes community oncology practices, where the majority of patients with LBCL receive treatment, reinforcing the potential for cema-cel to expand CAR T access beyond traditional treatment centers.

Early clinical experience has demonstrated the feasibility of outpatient cema-cel administration, including at community practices with limited or no prior CAR T experience. Together with the integration of MRD testing to identify patients at risk of relapse, these findings support the potential for ALPHA3 to establish a new treatment paradigm that brings CAR T earlier in the course of disease and closer to where patients receive care.




The Company continues to anticipate its next clinical update in mid-2027, with trial enrollment expected to be completed by year-end 2027.

Cema-Cel: Advancing Commercial Readiness and Patient Access
As the ALPHA3 program advances, Allogene has begun laying the groundwork for potential commercialization of cema-cel, with an early focus on payer coverage, reimbursement and treatment-site readiness to support broader patient access. To lead these efforts, the Company appointed Rocio Manghani, MPH, as Vice President, Market Access, Evidence & Strategy. Ms. Manghani brings extensive cell therapy commercialization experience from Kite, Gamida Cell and Orca Bio, including expertise in payer coverage, reimbursement, evidence generation and launch operations. Her appointment underscores Allogene's commitment to addressing access barriers early and preparing to bring CAR T to patients where they receive care.

Further informing this strategy, an abstract accepted for online publication in November in connection with the 2026 American Society of Hematology (ASH) Annual Meeting and in the supplemental issue of Blood will highlight findings from a survey of 152 U.S. hematologists and oncologists examining barriers to CAR T adoption and opportunities to expand access through allogeneic therapy.

Building on these insights, Allogene plans to host an in-person event at ASH exploring how cema-cel could help overcome longstanding barriers to CAR T adoption. The discussion will explore how earlier intervention guided by MRD testing, combined with the potential for treatment in community practices, could expand access to CAR T. Additional event details will be announced at a later date.

ALLO-329: Purpose-Built Allogeneic CAR T for Autoimmune Disease
ALLO-329 is a next-generation, dual-targeting anti-CD19/CD70 AlloCAR T product incorporating the Company’s proprietary Dagger® technology. The product was designed to address allogeneic rejection by targeting activated CD70-positive patient T cells, with the goal of optimizing the benefit-risk profile while reducing or eliminating the need for conventional chemotherapy-based lymphodepletion (LD).

The ongoing Phase 1 RESOLUTION trial is a dose-escalation study evaluating ALLO-329 across multiple autoimmune indications, including systemic sclerosis (scleroderma), idiopathic inflammatory myopathies (myositis) or systemic lupus erythematosus (lupus). The trial incorporates a parallel evaluation of three conditioning approaches: no LD, cyclophosphamide alone and fludarabine plus cyclophosphamide. The design of RESOLUTION is intended to help distinguish the contribution of ALLO-329 from that of chemotherapy-based conditioning and inform selection of a treatment regimen that minimizes the burden and toxicity of LD without compromising efficacy.

Allogene will present updated clinical and translational data from RESOLUTION at the 2026 American College of Rheumatology (ACR) Convergence on November 9, 2026. The presentation will build upon the published nine-patient abstract with an expanded patient population, longer follow-up and results across three dose levels and three LD approaches.

The expanded dataset will provide a more comprehensive assessment of ALLO-329's emerging safety and efficacy, with a focus on scleroderma, while offering new insights into the relationship between conditioning intensity and treatment response. Enrollment and dose escalation continue as the Company evaluates additional dose levels and conditioning approaches to inform selection of a potential Phase 2 regimen.

Allogene will host a webinar on November 9, 2026, at 6:00 a.m. PT/9:00 a.m. ET to review the updated clinical and translational findings, prior to the ACR poster presentation later that day.

Next-Generation Pipeline: Advancing the Dagger® Platform
Allogene will highlight its leadership in allogeneic CAR T at the Society for Immunotherapy of Cancer (SITC) Annual Meeting, November 4–8, 2026, through two invited presentations and a preclinical poster showcasing the breadth and continued evolution of its platform.

On November 5, Jeff McLeroy, M.D., Vice President, Head of Safety and Pharmacovigilance, discussed clinical findings from the Phase 1 TRAVERSE trial of ALLO-316 in renal cell carcinoma, including evidence of durable responses, CAR T expansion and tumor infiltration in patients with advanced disease. On November 6, Dr. Roberts will present Allogene's broader vision for allogeneic CAR T as a platform designed to improve patient access across oncology and autoimmune disease, highlighting the Company's clinical programs and the potential to bring cell therapy into more treatment settings.

Complementing these presentations, the Company will present preclinical data evaluating a novel off-the-shelf DLL3/CD70 dual CAR T product candidate for small cell lung cancer and other neuroendocrine tumors. The findings suggest that careful selection of the genomic location for site-specific-integration of the CAR can improve CAR T-cell performance in controlling tumors. Confirming and extending observations presented earlier this year with BCMA, the data show pairing the DLL3 CAR



with a CAR targeting CD70 can enhance resistance to the immune-mediated rejection, illustrating that Dagger® technology can be coupled with other CARs. These findings reinforce the potential of the Dagger technology as a versatile foundation for Allogene's next-generation pipeline, supporting the development of off-the-shelf CAR T therapies across additional targets for both hematological and solid tumors as well as autoimmune disease.

About Allogene Therapeutics
Allogene Therapeutics, with headquarters in South San Francisco, is a clinical-stage biotechnology company pioneering the development of allogeneic chimeric antigen receptor T cell (AlloCAR T) products for cancer and autoimmune disease. Led by cell therapy veterans applying proven CAR T experience, Allogene is developing a pipeline of off-the-shelf CAR T cell product candidates with the goal of delivering readily available cell therapy on-demand, more reliably, and at greater scale to more patients.
