Getty Images Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
•Secured Support of All Major Financial Stakeholder Groups on Comprehensive Restructuring Transactions, including up to $533 Million Equity Commitment from Getty Family and Koch Equity Development
•Initiated Prepackaged Chapter 11 Process to Implement Agreed-Upon Plan of Reorganization with No Impact on Operations, Employees, Contributors, Partners, Customers, or Vendors

New York, NY, November 16, 2026 – Getty Images Holdings, Inc. (“Getty Images” or the “Company”) (OTCPK: GETYQ), the preeminent source of visual content and coverage, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

“As recently announced, we initiated a financial restructuring to significantly strengthen our financial foundation and accelerate our ability to invest for growth with the support of the Getty family, Koch Equity Development and our lenders as a privately held company,” said Craig Peters, Chief Executive Officer of Getty Images. “We continue to service our customers through the world's best authentic visual content and coverage. Importantly, we remain focused on what we do best, which is helping our customers engage their audiences.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary:
•Revenue of $234.0 million in Q3’26, a decrease of 2.5% year over year and on a currency neutral basis. Excluding Creative Agency* and iStock e-commerce, enterprise-focused offerings grew 3.5% year over year and 3.4% on a currency neutral basis, reflecting continued demand for the Company’s Corporate, Media and Editorial offerings despite ongoing headwinds in Agency and iStock. To address these pressures the Company continued reallocating resources toward Corporate, Media and Editorial offerings where it sees stronger customer demand and greater long-term opportunity, accompanied by a reduction in marketing spend in areas where returns no longer met targets.
•Creative revenue of $123.0 million, down 15.1% year over year and on a currency neutral basis. The decline was driven primarily by lower revenue from iStock e-commerce, double-digit declines in the Creative Agency* business, and a shift in revenue allocation from Creative to Editorial within Premium Access offerings due to strong demand for event-driven content such as the FIFA World Cup, partially offset by growth in Custom Content.
•Editorial revenue of $105.6 million, up 18.2% year over year and 18.5% on a currency neutral basis, with the performance driven primarily by Sport, with additional contributions from the News, Archive, and Entertainment verticals. Editorial performance also reflects the shift in revenue allocation within Premium Access offerings driven by the higher demand for event-driven editorial content.
•Other revenue of $5.5 million, down $0.4 million from $5.8 million in Q3'25.
•Annual Subscription Revenue was 56.3% of total revenue, compared to 58.4% in Q3’25.
•Net loss of $564.5 million in Q3’26, compared to a net income of $21.6 million in Q3’25. Primary drivers of the year-on-year decrease include:
•$583.8 million decrease in income from operations driven primarily by $563.2 million non-cash goodwill and indefinite-lived trade name impairment charges and $26.1 million of restructuring charges,
•$12.1 million increase in loss from retirement of debt,
•$6.0 million increase in interest expense primarily due to the higher rates following the 2025 refinancing transactions,
•$1.9 million improvement in foreign exchange gain primarily due to revaluation of the Euro Term Loan, and
•$13.5 million increase in tax benefit primarily due to a change in valuation allowance, impairment of goodwill and indefinite-lived trade name, and non-deductible interest.
•Net loss margin for Q3’26 was 241.2% compared to net income margin of 9.0% in Q3’25.


•On a non-GAAP basis, adjusted net loss* was $109.7 million in Q3'26, compared to $33.5 million adjusted net income** in the prior year period.
•Adjusted EBITDA** was $76.6 million for Q3’26, down 2.6% year over year and 2.8% on a currency neutral basis, primarily reflecting lower revenue.
•Adjusted EBITDA margin** was 32.7% for Q3’26 compared to 32.8% in the prior year period.
•Adjusted EBITDA less capex** was $63.7 million, down 0.4% year over year and 0.9% on a currency neutral basis.

Liquidity and Balance Sheet:
•Net cash used in operating activities was $9.1 million in Q3’26, compared to net cash provided by operating activities of $22.6 million in the prior year period.
•Free cash flow* was $(22.0) million in Q3’26, compared to $7.9 million in the prior year period, with the decline primarily driven by a $16.3 million increase in cash interest paid, including $10.1 million of interest on the 10.5% Senior Secured Notes tied to the financing of the terminated merger with Shutterstock, and a $4.2 million partial payment of judgment and interest related to the warrant litigation, highlighting the impact of the financial obligations addressed by the restructuring transactions described below.
•Ending cash balance was $66.2 million as of September 30, 2026, down $24.0 million from December 31, 2025 and down $43.3 million from September 30, 2025.
•Total debt was $1.5 billion as of September 30, 2026, which included $539.9 million in Senior Secured Notes; Term Loan balance of $501.7 million, consisting of $40.1 million in USD and $461.6 million in USD equivalent of Euros, converted using exchange rates as of September 30, 2026; $270.0 million of Senior Unsecured Notes; and $150.0 million borrowed under the revolving credit facility, including $30 million drawn from the facility in July.
•Following termination of the Merger Agreement in July 2026, the $628.4 million of 10.5% Senior Secured Notes were redeemed at par in accordance with a special mandatory redemption pursuant to the indenture, with the redemption funded by amounts released from escrow.

* Agency is based on booked revenue, which is a good approximation for recognized Agency revenue given Agency is predominantly a la carte.

Chapter 11 Proceedings and Restructuring Transactions
On October 12, 2026, the Company and certain of its subsidiaries (the “Company Parties”) entered into a Restructuring Support Agreement (the “RSA”) with the Company’s majority shareholders and holders of approximately 89% of the aggregate outstanding principal amount of its first lien term loans and senior secured notes, 100% of its revolving loans and approximately 83.5% of its senior unsecured notes, providing for a comprehensive recapitalization to be implemented through a prepackaged Chapter 11 plan of reorganization. The recapitalization contemplates, among other things: (i) an equity commitment of approximately $533.0 million by the majority shareholders, inclusive of debtor-in-possession term loans provided by certain of the Company's majority shareholders and approximately $100.0 million of prepetition debt held by the majority shareholders that will convert into equity upon emergence; (ii) the conversion of the first lien term loans and senior secured notes held by holders other than the majority shareholders into new first lien debt with extended maturities and a reduced interest rate, and the conversion of the revolving loans into an amended and restated $150.0 million revolving credit facility, that will be repaid and undrawn at emergence; (iii) the retirement of all of the senior unsecured notes in exchange for an aggregate cash payment of approximately $170.2 million; and (iv) the cancellation of all existing equity interests in the Company, with the majority shareholders owning 100% of the reorganized company upon emergence, subject to dilution by a management incentive plan.

On October 21, 2026, the Company Parties commenced voluntary prepackaged cases (the "Chapter 11 Cases") under Chapter 11 of title 11 of the United States Code (the "Bankruptcy Code") in the United States Bankruptcy Court for


the District of Delaware (the "Bankruptcy Court") to implement the restructuring transactions contemplated by the RSA and the related prepackaged plan of reorganization. In connection with the Chapter 11 Cases, the Company obtained interim approval of debtor-in-possession financing in an aggregate principal amount of up to $143.0 million and, on October 23, 2026, borrowed $85.0 million under that facility.

About Getty Images
Getty Images (OTCPK: GETYQ) is the preeminent source of authentic visual content and coverage that offers a full range of content solutions to meet the needs of any customer around the globe, no matter their size. Through its Getty Images, iStock and Unsplash brands, websites and APIs, Getty Images serves customers in almost every country in the world and is the first-place people turn to discover, purchase and share powerful visual content from the world’s best photographers and videographers. Getty Images works with over 600,000 content creators and over 360 content partners to deliver this powerful and comprehensive content. Each year Getty Images covers more than 160,000 news, sport and entertainment events providing depth and breadth of coverage that is unmatched. Getty Images maintains one of the largest and best privately-owned photographic archives in the world with millions of images dating back to the beginning of photography.

Through its best-in-class creative library and Custom Content solutions, Getty Images helps customers elevate their creativity and entire end‑to‑end creative process to find the right visual for any need. With the adoption and distribution of generative AI technologies and tools trained on permissioned content that include indemnification and perpetual, worldwide usage rights, Getty Images and iStock customers can use text to image generation to ideate and create commercially safe compelling visuals, further expanding Getty Images capabilities to deliver exactly what customers are looking for.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

In order to assist investors in understanding the core operating results that our management uses to evaluate the business and for financial planning, we present the following non-GAAP measures: (1) Adjusted EBITDA, (2) Adjusted EBITDA margin, (3) Adjusted EBITDA less capex (4) Adjusted EBITDA less capex margin, (5) Adjusted net income, (6) Adjusted earnings per share and (7) Free cash flow. The presentation of this financial information is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, the financial information prepared and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP.

The Company believes that these measures are relevant and provide useful information widely used by analysts, investors and other interested parties in our industry to provide a baseline for evaluating and comparing our operating performance, and in the case of free cash flow, our liquidity results. We also evaluate our revenue and other metrics on an as reported (U.S. GAAP) and currency neutral basis. Currency neutral revenue changes (expressed as a percentage) exclude the impact of fluctuating foreign currency values pegged to the U.S. Dollar between comparative periods by translating all local currencies using the current period exchange rates. We consistently apply this approach to revenue for all countries where the functional currency is not the U.S. Dollar. We believe presenting currency neutral information provides valuable supplemental information regarding our comparable results, consistent with how we evaluate our performance internally.
