Wynn Resorts, Limited Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

LAS VEGAS, November 5, 2026 — Wynn Resorts, Limited (NASDAQ: WYNN) ("Wynn Resorts" or the "Company") today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Operating revenues were $1.69 billion for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $144.7 million from $1.83 billion for the third quarter of 2025. Net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited was $17.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited of $88.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. Diluted net income per share was $0.17 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to diluted net income per share of $0.85 for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Property EBITDAR(1) was $464.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to Adjusted Property EBITDAR of $570.1 million for the third quarter of 2025.

“Our third quarter results demonstrated strong operational discipline from the teams in each of our regions,” said Craig Billings, CEO of Wynn Resorts, Limited. “Wynn Las Vegas and our Macau properties both saw healthy demand amid slower market conditions. Wynn Las Vegas was also negatively impacted by a hold rate outside of our expected range. In Boston, the team did an excellent job managing through the strike that began in early September, ensuring that we could continue delighting guests at our resort. The team at Wynn Al Marjan Island has also made substantial progress with ongoing finishing works occurring throughout the property, including the interior fit-out of all guest rooms, as we move closer to a planned September 2027 opening.”

Consolidated Results
Operating revenues were $1.69 billion for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $144.7 million from $1.83 billion for the third quarter of 2025. For the third quarter of 2026, operating revenues decreased $51.7 million, $45.8 million, $30.1 million and $17.1 million at Wynn Palace, our Las Vegas Operations, Wynn Macau, and Encore Boston Harbor, respectively, from the third quarter of 2025.

Net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited was $17.3 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited of $88.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. Diluted net income per share was $0.17 for the third quarter of 2026, compared to diluted net income per share of $0.85 for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited(2) was $33.9 million, or $0.33 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited of $88.7 million, or $0.86 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025.

Adjusted Property EBITDAR was $464.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $105.9 million compared to Adjusted Property EBITDAR of $570.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. For the third quarter of 2026, Adjusted Property EBITDAR decreased $44.6 million, $34.5 million, $18.5 million, and $8.3 million at our Las Vegas Operations, Wynn Palace, Wynn Macau, and Encore Boston Harbor, respectively, from the third quarter of 2025.

Wynn Resorts, Limited also announced on November 5, 2026 that its Board of Directors declared a cash dividend of $0.25 per share, payable on November 30, 2026 to stockholders of record as of November 16, 2026.

Property Results
Macau Operations
Wynn Palace
Operating revenues from Wynn Palace were $583.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $51.7 million from $635.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Property EBITDAR from Wynn Palace was $165.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $200.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. Table games win percentage in mass market operations was 25.8%, above the 22.6% experienced in the third quarter of 2025. VIP table games win as a percentage of turnover was 3.82%, above the property's expected range of 3.1% to 3.4% and below the 4.68% experienced in the third quarter of 2025.

Wynn Macau
Operating revenues from Wynn Macau were $335.4 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $30.1 million from $365.5 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Property EBITDAR from Wynn Macau was $89.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $108.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. Table games win percentage in mass market operations was 17.1%, below the 18.7% experienced in the third quarter of 2025. VIP table games win as a percentage of turnover was 4.88%, above the property's expected range of 3.1% to 3.4% and above the 2.93% experienced in the third quarter of 2025.

Las Vegas Operations
Operating revenues from our Las Vegas Operations were $575.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $45.8 million from $621.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Property EBITDAR from our Las Vegas Operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $158.8 million, compared to $203.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Table games win percentage for the third quarter of 2026 was 18.9%, below the property's expected range of 22% to 26% and below the 23.0% experienced in the third quarter of 2025.

Encore Boston Harbor
Operating revenues from Encore Boston Harbor were $194.7 million for the third quarter of 2026, a decrease of $17.1 million from $211.8 million for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted Property EBITDAR from Encore Boston Harbor for the third quarter of 2026 was $50.1 million, compared to $58.4 million for the third quarter of 2025. Table games win percentage for the third quarter of 2026 was 18.9%, within the property's expected range of 18% to 22% and below the 20.5% experienced in the third quarter of 2025.

Wynn Al Marjan Island Development
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company contributed $62.2 million of cash to the 40%-owned joint venture that is constructing the Wynn Al Marjan Island development in the UAE, bringing our life-to-date cash contributions to the project to $1.12 billion. Wynn Al Marjan Island is currently expected to open in September 2027.

Balance Sheet
Our cash and cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 totaled $1.64 billion, excluding $150.2 million of short-term investments held by Wynn Macau, Limited ("WML"). Cash and cash equivalents is comprised of $1.15 billion held by WML and subsidiaries, $371.5 million held by Wynn Resorts Finance, LLC ("WRF") and subsidiaries excluding WML, and $120.3 million held at Corporate and other. As of September 30, 2026, the available borrowing capacity under the WRF Revolver and the WM Cayman II Revolver was $1.03 billion and $1.35 billion, respectively.

Total current and long-term debt outstanding at September 30, 2026 was $10.73 billion, comprised of $5.77 billion of Macau-related debt, $4.37 billion of WRF debt, and $599.1 million of debt held by the retail joint venture which we consolidate.

Recent Debt Financing Activities

In September 2026, WRF and its subsidiary Wynn Resorts Capital Corp. issued $900.0 million aggregate principal amount of 6.875% Senior Notes due 2035 and used the net proceeds from the offering, along with cash on hand, to redeem in full an aggregate principal amount of $900.0 million of Wynn Las Vegas 5.250% Senior Notes due 2027.

Equity Repurchase Program

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased 627,475 shares of its common stock under its publicly announced equity repurchase program at an average price of $92.61 per share, for an aggregate cost of $58.1 million. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $268.0 million in repurchase authority remaining under the equity repurchase program.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
(1) "Adjusted Property EBITDAR" is net income before interest, income taxes, depreciation and amortization, pre-opening expenses, property charges and other expenses, triple-net operating lease rent expense related to Encore Boston Harbor, management and license fees, corporate expenses and other expenses (including intercompany golf course, meeting and convention, and water rights leases), stock-based compensation, change in derivatives fair value, loss on debt financing transactions and other non-operating income and expenses. Adjusted Property EBITDAR is presented exclusively as a supplemental disclosure because management believes that it is widely used to measure the performance, and as a basis for valuation, of gaming companies. Management uses Adjusted Property EBITDAR as a measure of the operating performance of its segments and to compare the operating performance of its properties with those of its competitors, as well as a basis for determining certain incentive compensation. We also present Adjusted Property EBITDAR because it is used by some investors to measure a company's ability to incur and service debt, make capital expenditures and meet working capital requirements. Gaming companies have historically reported EBITDAR as a supplement to GAAP. In order to view the operations of their casinos on a more stand-alone basis, gaming companies, including us, have historically excluded from their EBITDAR calculations pre-opening expenses, property charges, corporate expenses and stock-based compensation, that do not relate to the management of specific casino properties. However, Adjusted Property EBITDAR should not be considered as an alternative to operating income (loss) as an indicator of our performance, as an alternative to cash flows from operating activities as a measure of liquidity, or as an alternative to any other measure determined in accordance with GAAP. Unlike net income, Adjusted Property EBITDAR does not include depreciation or interest expense and therefore does not reflect current or future capital expenditures or the cost of capital. We have significant uses of cash flows, including capital expenditures, triple-net operating lease rent expense related to Encore Boston Harbor, interest payments, debt principal repayments, income taxes and other non-recurring charges, which are not reflected in Adjusted Property EBITDAR. Also, our calculation of Adjusted Property EBITDAR may be different from the calculation methods used by other companies and, therefore, comparability may be limited.

(2) "Adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited" is net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited before pre-opening expenses, property charges and other expenses, change in derivatives fair value, loss on debt financing transactions, foreign currency remeasurement and other, and income taxes calculated using the specific tax treatment applicable to the adjustments based on their respective jurisdictions. Adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited and adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited per diluted share are presented as supplemental disclosures to financial measures in accordance with GAAP because management believes that these non-GAAP financial measures are widely used to measure the performance, and as a principal basis for valuation, of gaming companies. These measures are used by management and/or evaluated by some investors, in addition to net income per share computed in accordance with GAAP, as an additional basis for assessing period-to-period results of our business. Adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited and adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited per diluted share may be different from the calculation methods used by other companies and, therefore, comparability may be limited.

The Company has included schedules in the tables that accompany this release that reconcile (i) net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited to adjusted net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited, (ii) operating income (loss) to Adjusted Property EBITDAR, and (iii) net income attributable to Wynn Resorts, Limited to Adjusted Property EBITDAR.
